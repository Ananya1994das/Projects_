# Projects-
# 🏨 Hotel Booking Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **119,390 hotel booking records** from **City Hotels and Resort Hotels** to uncover meaningful insights into booking patterns, customer behavior, cancellations, pricing, and revenue trends.

The primary objective is to understand the key factors influencing **booking cancellations and Average Daily Rate (ADR)** and translate the findings into actionable business recommendations. By analyzing historical booking data, the project identifies seasonal demand patterns, customer preferences, length of stay, market segments, geographical trends, and pricing behavior.

Using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib, and Plotly**, the project performs comprehensive **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** including data cleaning, missing-value analysis, descriptive statistics, categorical analysis, correlation analysis, bivariate and multivariate analysis, time-based trend analysis, and outlier detection.

### 🎯 Key Objectives

* Analyze booking and cancellation patterns across City and Resort Hotels.
* Identify seasonal trends and peak booking periods.
* Understand customer demographics, preferences, and booking behavior.
* Analyze factors affecting **Average Daily Rate (ADR)**.
* Examine market segments, distribution channels, meal preferences, and geographical patterns.
* Identify potential reasons for high cancellation rates.
* Generate actionable recommendations to improve **occupancy, revenue, customer retention, and cancellation management**.

### 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis identified several important patterns, including significant cancellation activity, strong seasonal demand, higher booking volumes for City Hotels, low repeat-guest participation, and variations in ADR across hotel types and booking periods.

These insights can help hotel management make more informed decisions around **seasonal pricing, promotional strategies, cancellation policies, customer retention, and Resort Hotel packages**.

**Tools & Technologies:** Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Plotly | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

**Project Type:** Exploratory Data Analysis / Business Analytics
